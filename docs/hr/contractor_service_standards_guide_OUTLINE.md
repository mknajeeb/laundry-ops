# Contractor Service Standards Guide — Outline (for review)

**Target length:** 6–8 pages when formatted  
**Audience:** 1099 independent contractors  
**Companion documents:** Independent Contractor Agreement · Contractor Performance Standards & Benchmarks Addendum

**Principle:** Service relationship and process. Not employee language. **Qualitative expectations** in signed addendum; **numeric targets** in systems. Coaching/warnings/stop-assignments by **email** + HR Timeline.

---

## Cover

- VeeWash LLC
- Effective date
- Works with IC Agreement; not an employee

---

## 1. Independent contractor relationship (~½ page)

- IC status; no guaranteed assignments or employee benefits  
- W-9 required; taxes contractor responsibility  
- Perform **accepted** service per Agreement, this guide, and supervisor direction

---

## 2. Official company communications (~½ page)

**Required text (locked):**

Contractors agree that company-provided email, personal email on file, company software notifications, text messages, and written notices may be used for:

- Scheduling  
- Payroll communications  
- Policy updates  
- Coaching  
- Warnings  
- Performance discussions  
- Assignment changes  
- Separation notices  

Workers are responsible for maintaining current contact information and reviewing communications sent through approved channels.

- Service coaching, warnings, and stop-assignments may be sent by **email**  
- No separate signed service warning forms

---

## 3. Assignments & reliability — overview (~½ page)

- Accept and complete agreed assignments  
- Clock when required for payment records  
- **Operational call-out / tardiness rules** → Contractor Performance Addendum  
- Cross-reference discipline Section 9

---

## 4. Payment & records (~½ page)

- Payment per Agreement and rate terms  
- Review payment summaries; report errors promptly  
- Accurate records; falsification → immediate stop of assignments  
- Does not replace W-9 / tax obligations

## 9. Performance process — discipline framework (~2 pages) — LOCKED

**Language:** Service warning / stop offering assignments (not “termination” unless legally required in email).

Mirror employee framework:

### 9.1 Late arrival (accepted assignment)

- First → **Coaching email**  
- Second → **Warning / service notice email**  
- Continued pattern → **Stop assignments** at management discretion  

### 9.2 Pattern of tardiness

- **Warning email** → continued → stop assignments  

### 9.3 No-call / no-show (accepted work)

- Immediate stop of assignments possible; no prior warning required  

### 9.4 Pattern of attendance / reliability issues

- **Warning email** → continued → stop assignments (Separation – Attendance template adapted for contractor)  

### 9.5 Performance

- **Warning email** → continued → stop assignments  

### 9.6 Customer item care / quality

- **Warning email** → continued → stop assignments; direct stop if severity warrants  

### 9.7 Serious incidents

Immediate stop of assignments without prior warning. Same serious-incident list as employee handbook.

### 9.8 Not used

Final service warning forms, probation, PIP, cure notices, suspension forms, first-time packet discipline forms.
