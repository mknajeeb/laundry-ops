# VeeWash — Contractor Performance Standards & Benchmarks Addendum

**VeeWash LLC**  
Effective date: _______________

This addendum applies to **independent contractors (1099)** performing services for VeeWash / WashPro. It works with your **Independent Contractor Agreement** and **Contractor Service Standards Guide**.

**Numeric service targets** are **not** fixed in this document. They are established and updated by management in **operational systems** and supervisor communication.

You are **not an employee**. This addendum does not create employment status or guaranteed assignments.

---

## Acknowledgement of management-established targets

Contractors acknowledge that **operational targets, productivity standards, quality standards, and performance metrics** for accepted service may be **established, revised, and communicated by management from time to time** through approved channels, posted guidance, or company systems.

Meeting those targets is part of service expectations. **Changing a numeric target does not require a new signed addendum.**

---

## 1. Attendance & Reliability

### Expectations

- **Arrive on time** for accepted assignments and scheduled service periods.
- **Follow the call-out procedure** before failing to perform accepted work.
- **Maintain reliable attendance** for work you have accepted.

### Call-out procedure (accepted assignments)

1. Contact your **designated supervisor or manager** **at least 2 hours before** the accepted assignment start when possible.
2. Use an **approved channel**: company text line, supervisor phone, company email, or in-app notification as directed.
3. State that you **cannot perform the accepted assignment**, the **date/time**, and whether you can accept other work if offered.
4. If you cannot give 2 hours notice (emergency), **notify immediately** when you know you cannot perform accepted work.
5. Failure to notify management is not an acceptable call-out.

### No-call / no-show policy

A **no-call / no-show** is failing to perform **accepted work** without following the call-out procedure.

- **One no-call / no-show** for accepted work is a **serious issue** and may result in **stopping future assignments immediately** without prior service warning, at management discretion.

### Tardiness expectations

- **On time** means **clocked in and ready to perform accepted service** at the agreed start time (or assigned station).
- **Tardy** means arriving or clocking in after start without management approval.
- **First late arrival:** coaching email + HR Timeline entry.
- **Second late arrival:** warning / service notice email + HR Timeline entry.
- **Continued tardiness or pattern:** assignments may stop at management discretion.

### Reliability

- Perform **accepted assignments** you have agreed to complete.
- Keep contact information current in company systems.

---

## 2. Productivity

### Expectations

- **Meet productivity standards established by management** for accepted service, role, and current operating conditions.
- **Maintain an acceptable pace of work** during clocked service time without sacrificing quality, safety, or customer item care.
- Follow supervisor direction on rush and priority work.

Management sets specific productivity expectations in **company systems and direct communication**. Those expectations may change without a new signed addendum.

**Sustained underperformance** after coaching may lead to a service warning and stopping assignments.

---

## 3. Quality

### Expectations

- **Perform work accurately** — correct sort, fold, label, and processing steps.
- **Minimize rework and errors**; correct mistakes before work is released when possible.
- Follow training and posted standards.

Management may set quality metrics in **operational systems**. Metrics may be revised without a new signed addendum.

**Repeated errors or careless rework** may lead to a service warning and stopping assignments.

---

## 4. Customer Item Care

### Expectations

- **Protect customer property** during accepted service.
- **Maintain order integrity** — keep each customer’s order separated.
- **Follow customer instructions** on labels, tickets, and service notes.
- Report missing, damaged, or questionable items **immediately** to management.

**Mixed orders**, confirmed losses due to negligence, and careless damage are **serious** issues and may result in stopping assignments without prior warning.

---

## 5. Conduct & Professionalism

### Expectations

- **Follow instructions** from supervisors for accepted service.
- **Work respectfully** with staff, other contractors, and customers.
- **Maintain professional conduct** on site.

Additional detail appears in the Contractor Service Standards Guide. Serious conduct failures may result in immediate stop of assignments.

---

## 6. Changes to this addendum

This document defines **service expectations and attendance policies**. If management materially changes these **written expectations**, an updated addendum may be issued.

**Numeric operational targets** are updated in **Performance Settings and production systems** without requiring a new signed addendum, as acknowledged above.
