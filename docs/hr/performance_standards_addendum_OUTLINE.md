**Two versions:** Employee · Contractor (same structure, lane-specific wording)

**Target length:** 2–4 pages each when formatted  
**Signed once** at onboarding (re-sign only if **written expectation/policy text** in addendum materially changes — **not** when numeric targets change)

---

## Three-layer model (locked)

| Layer | Content | Changes without new signature? |
|---|---|---|
| **Handbook / Service Guide** | Policies, discipline, communications | Rare; handbook stable |
| **This addendum** | Performance **expectations** + **attendance policies** | Only if expectation text changes |
| **Performance Settings / dashboards** | **Numeric targets** (bags/hr, quality %, etc.) | **Yes** — editable anytime |

**Do not include in signed addendum:** 2.5 bags/hr, 40 lbs/hr, 24 min/bag, step-time seconds, 98% issue-free, or any fixed operational numbers.

## Acknowledgement of management-established targets (required)

Workers acknowledge that operational targets, productivity standards, quality standards, and performance metrics may be **established, revised, and communicated by management from time to time**. Meeting those targets is required. **Changing a numeric target does not require a new signed addendum.**

Repeat on signature page.

---

## Section 1 — Attendance & Reliability (specific policies — keep in addendum)

**Expectations (qualitative):**

- Arrive on time  
- Follow call-out procedure  
- Maintain reliable attendance  

**Policies (specific):**

- Call-out procedure (2 hours when possible, approved channels, emergency notify)  
- No-call / no-show definition and consequence (immediate separation / stop assignments possible)  
- Tardiness definition (on time vs tardy; pattern → discipline framework)  
- Reliability (contact info, accept work)

**No numeric productivity targets in this section.**

---

## Section 2 — Productivity (expectations only)

- Meet productivity standards **established by management**  
- Maintain acceptable pace of work  
- Rush/priority when directed  
- Targets live in Performance Settings / dashboards / supervisor communication  
- Sustained underperformance → warning path  

---

## Section 3 — Quality (expectations only)

- Perform work accurately  
- Minimize rework and errors  
- Quality metrics may be set in systems; revisable without re-signature  
- Repeated errors → warning path  

---

## Section 4 — Customer Item Care (expectations only)

- Protect customer property  
- Maintain order integrity  
- Follow customer instructions  
- Report problems immediately  
- Mixed orders / negligent loss → serious incident path  

---

## Section 5 — Conduct & Professionalism (expectations only)

- Follow instructions  
- Work respectfully  
- Maintain professional conduct  
- Pointer to handbook / guide for detail  

---

## Section 6 — Changes to this addendum

- Re-issue addendum only when **written expectation text** changes  
- Numeric targets updated in systems only
