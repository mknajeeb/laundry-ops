# Employee Handbook — Outline (for review)

**Target length:** 8–10 pages when formatted  
**Audience:** W-2 and temporary W-2 employees  
**Companion document:** Performance Standards & Benchmarks Addendum (signed separately)

**Principle:** High-level policy and process. **Qualitative performance expectations** in signed addendum; **numeric targets** in Performance Settings / systems only. Discipline delivered by **email** + internal HR Timeline — no step forms.

---

## Cover

- VeeWash LLC / WashPro
- Effective date
- Applies to W-2 and temporary W-2 employees

## 3. Official company communications (~½ page)

**Required text (locked):**

Employees agree that company-provided email, personal email on file, company software notifications, text messages, and written notices may be used for:

- Scheduling  
- Payroll communications  
- Policy updates  
- Coaching  
- Warnings  
- Performance discussions  
- Assignment changes  
- Separation notices  

Workers are responsible for maintaining current contact information and reviewing communications sent through approved channels.

- Coaching, warnings, and separations may be sent by **email** using company templates
- No separate signed warning or coaching forms

## 6. How performance is measured (~½ page)

Five categories (table):

1. Attendance & Reliability  
2. Productivity  
3. Quality  
4. Customer Item Care  
5. Conduct & Professionalism  

- Numeric targets → **Addendum only**

## 7. Job standards — expectations (~1 page)

High-level only (no numbers):

- Productivity: engaged work, department pace, rush priority without sacrificing quality  
- Quality: correct sort, fold, label, low rework  
- Customer item care: separation of orders, report problems immediately  
- Safety on the floor: machines, chemicals, injuries  

→ “See Performance Standards & Benchmarks Addendum for current targets.”

## 11. Discipline framework (~2 pages) — LOCKED

### General

- Steps communicated by **email** (standard templates)
- Parallel internal **HR Timeline** entry by manager
- Management may skip steps for serious misconduct (Section 11.6)

### 11.1 Attendance — late arrival

| Occurrence | Action |
|---|---|
| **First** late arrival | **Coaching email** (template: Coaching – Late Arrival) |
| **Second** late arrival | **Warning email** (template: Warning – Pattern of Tardiness or Attendance / Reliability) |
| **Continued** tardiness / pattern | **Separation** at management discretion |

### 11.2 Attendance — pattern of tardiness

Examples: repeated lateness, habitual lateness, multiple late arrivals in a short period.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Warning email** (template: Warning – Pattern of Tardiness) |
| 2 | Continued behavior → **Separation** |

### 11.3 No-call / no-show

Failure to report for scheduled shift without notifying management.

- **Immediate separation** may occur at management discretion  
- **No warning required**

### 11.4 Pattern of attendance issues

Examples: repeated lateness, repeated absences, reliability concerns.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Warning email** (template: Warning – Attendance / Reliability) |
| 2 | Continued behavior → **Separation** (template: Separation – Attendance) |

### 11.5 Performance

Examples: consistent underperformance, failure to meet expectations, failure to follow procedures, repeated quality issues.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Warning email** (template: Warning – Performance) |
| 2 | Continued behavior → **Separation** (template: Separation – Performance) |

### 11.6 Customer item care / quality

Examples: order mix-up, lost item, negligent damage, failure to follow customer instructions, repeated processing errors.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Warning email** (template: Warning – Customer Item Care / Quality) |
| 2 | Continued behavior → **Separation** |
| Manager may proceed directly to separation if severity warrants |

### 11.7 Serious incidents — immediate separation

No prior warning required. Management retains full discretion.

Examples: theft, violence, harassment, falsifying records, intoxication, intentional damage, serious safety violation, gross negligence causing customer loss, major order contamination, significant customer property damage, **no-call / no-show**.

Separation communicated by email (template: Separation – Customer Item Care / Serious Incident, or Separation – Attendance as applicable).

### 11.8 What we do not use

- Final warning forms  
- PIP / improvement plans  
- Probation paperwork  
- Signed coaching or warning forms
