# VeeWash Contractor Service Standards Guide

**VeeWash LLC**  
Effective date: _______________

This guide applies to **independent contractors (1099)** performing services for VeeWash / WashPro. It works together with your **Independent Contractor Agreement**. The agreement covers legal and payment terms; **this guide covers how service is measured and managed on the floor.**

You are **not an employee**. This guide does not create employment status, guaranteed hours, or employee benefits.

---

## 1. Independent contractor relationship

- You are engaged as an **independent contractor**, not as an employee, partner, or agent of VeeWash LLC.
- The company may offer assignments; **acceptance of work** is subject to the Agreement and business needs. The company does not guarantee future assignments or a fixed schedule.
- You are responsible for your taxes and must provide a completed **Form W-9** before payment.
- Follow this guide, the Agreement, site rules communicated by management, and supervisor direction for **accepted assignments**.

---

## 2. Official company communications

Contractors agree that **company-provided email**, **personal email on file**, **company software notifications**, **text messages**, and **written notices** may be used for:

- Scheduling  
- Payroll and payment communications  
- Policy updates  
- Coaching  
- Warnings  
- Performance discussions  
- Assignment changes  
- Separation notices (including stop offering assignments)  

You are responsible for **maintaining current contact information** and **reviewing communications** sent through these approved channels.

---

## 3. Assignments, schedule, and reliability

**Attendance & Reliability** applies to accepted work.

- **Arrive on time** when you accept an assignment or scheduled service period.
- **Follow the call-out procedure** if you cannot perform accepted work. Contact management as early as possible through approved channels.
- **No-call / no-show** for accepted work is a **serious issue** and may result in stopping future assignments without further warning.
- **Accept assigned work** you have agreed to perform. Refusing accepted work without a valid safety or operational reason may be treated as a reliability and conduct issue.
- **Clock in and out** when required for payment records and site access. Clock records must be accurate.

---

## 4. Payment and records

- Payment terms are in your **Independent Contractor Agreement** and rate confirmation.
- Approved clock or service records may be used to prepare payment summaries or invoices.
- Review payment summaries promptly and report errors through approved channels.
- Falsifying time, service records, or payment documentation is grounds to stop assignments immediately.

This guide does not replace tax, invoice, or W-9 requirements.

---

## 5. How service performance is measured

Service is evaluated in **five categories** (same framework used for all workers on site):

| Category | What it means |
|---|---|
| **1. Attendance & Reliability** | On time, available for accepted work, proper call-outs |
| **2. Productivity** | Meets department productivity expectations for assigned service |
| **3. Quality** | Sorting, folding, processing, and bag handling with low rework |
| **4. Customer Item Care** | No lost items, no mixed orders, proper handling |
| **5. Conduct & Professionalism** | Follows instructions, professional behavior on site |

**Productivity and quality metrics** are established by management in **Performance Settings and operational systems** — not in this guide. The signed **Contractor Performance Standards & Benchmarks Addendum** defines service expectations; numeric targets may change without a new signature.

Repeated problems in any category may lead to coaching, a service warning, and stopping future assignments.

## 6. Service standards — laundry operations

These describe **expectations** for accepted service. **Attendance policies** and qualitative standards are in the **Contractor Performance Standards & Benchmarks Addendum**. **Numeric targets** are set in management systems.

### Productivity

- Work at the **pace required** for the assignment while maintaining quality and safety.
- Complete sorting, machine support, weighing, folding, and bag handling without unnecessary delay.
- Priority and rush work gets appropriate priority without sacrificing quality or customer item care.
- Sustained underperformance after coaching may lead to further action.

### Quality

- Sort and process items correctly by customer, order, and service type.
- Fold and present items per training and posted standards.
- Label, weigh, and bag orders correctly. Verify tickets and tags when required.
- Rework should be rare. Repeated rework is a quality issue.

### Customer item care

- Keep each customer order **separated**. Mixed orders are a serious issue.
- Handle garments and linens carefully. Report damage, stains, unusual items, or hazards immediately.
- Do not remove customer property from the premises except as authorized for accepted service.
- Do not make unauthorized commitments to customers about refunds, timing, or credits.

### Safety

- Work safely around machines, chemicals, hot surfaces, carts, and wet floors.
- Report injuries, near-misses, unsafe conditions, and incidents immediately.
- Do not operate equipment you are not trained to use. Do not bypass safety features.

---

## 7. Conduct, site rules, and customer property

**Conduct & Professionalism** applies on company premises and during accepted service.

- Follow supervisor instructions for workflow, quality, and safety.
- Treat staff, other contractors, and customers respectfully. No threats, harassment, or abusive conduct.
- No impairment, illegal drugs, or alcohol on premises. No weapons unless required by law.
- Use company equipment, supplies, and materials **only for accepted VeeWash service** — not personal or outside work without approval.
- Keep customer and company information confidential. Do not share order details, addresses, or pricing outside what is needed to perform service.
- Follow break, access, and restricted-area rules communicated on site.

---

## 8. Performance documentation

**You do not sign separate forms** for coaching, service warnings, performance discussions, or stop-offering-assignments notices.

The company documents service performance internally and may rely on:

- Emails and text messages through approved channels  
- System notes and HR timeline entries  
- Productivity and quality reports  
- Attendance and clock records  
- Payment and service records  
- Customer complaints  
- Manager observations  

Coaching and service warnings may be delivered **verbally** and/or through **approved written channels** (Section 2).

---

## 9. Performance process

When service standards are not met, management generally follows:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Coaching** — Clear explanation of the issue and expected correction |
| **2** | **Service warning** — Documented notice through approved channels that continued problems may result in stopping assignments |
| **3** | **Stop offering assignments** — End of the service relationship or cancellation of future work |

Management **may skip steps** for serious issues, including but not limited to:

- Theft or suspected theft  
- Violence, threats, or harassment  
- Falsifying records or time  
- Intoxication or impairment on site  
- Serious safety violations  
- Intentional damage to company or customer property  
- Confidentiality breaches or misuse of customer information  

Notices may be delivered through **approved communication channels** (Section 2). Payment for **approved service already performed** is handled per the Agreement.

There is no final-warning step, no probation review paperwork, and no separate cure or suspension forms in this system.
