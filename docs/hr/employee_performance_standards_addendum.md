# VeeWash / WashPro — Performance Standards & Benchmarks Addendum

**VeeWash LLC**  
Effective date: _______________

This addendum applies to **W-2 employees** and **temporary W-2 employees**. It works with the **Employee Handbook**. The handbook explains policies and discipline; **this addendum defines performance expectations** for your role.

**Numeric targets** (such as bags per hour, pounds per hour, quality percentages, and processing times) are **not** fixed in this document. They are established and updated by management in **operational systems** (Performance Settings, production dashboards, and supervisor communication).

---

## Acknowledgement of management-established targets

Employees acknowledge that **operational targets, productivity standards, quality standards, and performance metrics** may be **established, revised, and communicated by management from time to time** through approved channels, posted guidance, or company systems.

Meeting those targets is part of performance expectations. **Changing a numeric target does not require a new signed addendum.**

---

## 1. Attendance & Reliability

### Expectations

- **Arrive on time** for scheduled shifts.
- **Follow the call-out procedure** before missing work.
- **Maintain reliable attendance** and accept assigned work within your role.

### Call-out procedure

1. Contact your **direct supervisor** (or designated manager) **at least 2 hours before** your scheduled start time when possible.
2. Use an **approved channel**: company text line, supervisor phone, company email, or in-app notification as directed for your location.
3. State that you **cannot work the shift**, the **date**, and expected return if known.
4. If you cannot give 2 hours notice (emergency), **notify immediately** when you know you will miss work and explain briefly.
5. Do not assume a coworker’s message counts as your call-out. **You** must contact management.

### No-call / no-show policy

A **no-call / no-show** is missing a scheduled shift **without** following the call-out procedure above.

- **One no-call / no-show** is a **serious violation** and may result in **immediate separation** without prior warning, at management discretion.
- Pattern of unapproved absences is treated as an Attendance & Reliability failure.

### Tardiness expectations

- **On time** means **clocked in and ready to work** at your scheduled start time (or at the station your supervisor assigned).
- **Tardy** means clocking in after scheduled start without supervisor approval.
- **First late arrival:** coaching email + HR Timeline entry.
- **Second late arrival:** warning email + HR Timeline entry.
- **Continued tardiness or pattern of tardiness:** separation at management discretion.
- Habitual “just outside” lateness is treated as tardiness, not acceptable variance.

### Reliability

- Keep contact information current in company systems.
- Discuss safety concerns with your supervisor immediately rather than refusing work without notice.

---

## 2. Productivity

### Expectations

- **Meet productivity standards established by management** for your role, department, and current operating conditions.
- **Maintain an acceptable pace of work** during clocked productive time without sacrificing quality, safety, or customer item care.
- Follow supervisor direction on rush and priority orders.

### How targets are set

Management sets specific productivity expectations (for example throughput rates or processing pace) in **company systems and supervisor communication**. Those numbers may change as volume, equipment, or staffing changes.

**Sustained underperformance** against management-established standards, after coaching, may lead to a warning and separation.

---

## 3. Quality

### Expectations

- **Perform work accurately** — correct sort, fold, label, and processing steps.
- **Minimize rework and errors**; fix mistakes before orders are released when possible.
- Follow training and posted standards for presentation and handling.

Management may set quality metrics (for example error rates or issue-free percentages) in **operational systems**. Those metrics may be revised without a new signed addendum.

**Repeated errors or careless rework** may lead to a warning and separation.

---

## 4. Customer Item Care

### Expectations

- **Protect customer property** at all times.
- **Maintain order integrity** — keep each customer’s order separated through sort, fold, bag, and staging.
- **Follow customer instructions** on labels, tickets, tags, and service notes.
- Report missing, damaged, stained, unusual, or questionable items **immediately** to a supervisor.

**Mixed orders**, confirmed losses due to negligence, and careless damage are **serious** issues and may lead to immediate separation per the handbook.

---

## 5. Conduct & Professionalism

### Expectations

- **Follow instructions** from supervisors and managers for workflow, quality, and safety.
- **Work respectfully** with coworkers, supervisors, and customers.
- **Maintain professional conduct** on the floor and in break areas.

Additional detail appears in the Employee Handbook. Serious conduct failures may result in immediate separation.

---

## 6. Changes to this addendum

This document defines **performance expectations and attendance policies**. If management materially changes these **written expectations**, an updated addendum may be issued.

**Numeric operational targets** are updated in **Performance Settings and production systems** without requiring a new signed addendum, as acknowledged above.
